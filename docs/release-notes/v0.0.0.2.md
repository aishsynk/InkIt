**InkIt 0.0.0.2** - a big step up for trainers.

### New
- **Smart shapes:** draw a rough circle, box, triangle or arrow and it becomes a clean shape. A "V" at the end of a line makes it an arrow. Undo once keeps your freehand drawing.
- **Record a lesson:** the whole screen or an area, with your drawings, pointer and voice, saved as an MP4 in Videos > InkIt.
- **Pages and PDF handouts:** several whiteboard pages (Page Up / Page Down), export them as one PDF, or save and reopen them later.
- **PowerPoint:** during a slide show each slide keeps its own drawings.
- **Second screen:** show the screen, an area or one window on the projector.
- **Focus box**, **stamps** (✓ ✗ ? ! ★), **sticky notes** and **handwriting to text**.
- **For experts:** keys 1-6 for colours, [ ] for thickness, right-click tool wheel, two-finger tap to undo, a shortcut for any feature, and inkit:// links for Stream Deck.
- **Quick tour** on first start, **Send feedback** with your version filled in, and an optional **update check**.

### Try this
1. Draw a wobbly circle with the pen - it turns into a clean circle.
2. Press Ctrl+Shift+5, drag a box, and draw on the enlarged area.
3. Click **Record**, choose **Whole screen**, explain something, then **Stop**.
